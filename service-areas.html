Based in Port Coquitlam since 2003, OnTime Moving serves every city across Metro Vancouver — residential, commercial, packing, and storage.

Our Home Base

Movers in Port Coquitlam & Coquitlam

OnTime Moving has been based in Port Coquitlam since 2003. Our warehouse and storage facility is located at 104-1525 Broadway St — we know every neighbourhood, every street, and every strata building in the Tri-Cities area.

Residential Moving — Port Coquitlam

From Hyde Creek to Citadel Heights, our crews move Port Coquitlam families of every size — apartment, townhouse, or detached home. Free in-home estimate, written price, no surprises.

Storage Containers — Coquitlam & Port Coquitlam

Container delivered to your driveway, loaded at your pace, stored at our Port Coquitlam facility. 100 individual locked containers, 24/7 camera-monitored. Month-to-month rental available.

Coquitlam & Port Moody Moves

Serving Burke Mountain, Westwood Plateau, Heritage Mountain, and all of Coquitlam and Port Moody. We handle elevator bookings, long carries, and tight-corridor condo moves.

Burnaby

Movers in Burnaby

Burnaby is one of our most active service areas — from high-rise apartments at Brentwood and Metrotown to family homes in South Burnaby, the Heights, and Capitol Hill.

Apartment & Condo Moving — Burnaby

Elevator booking, floor protection, and tight-corridor handling. We've moved hundreds of Burnaby condo residents, from Brentwood Tower to Edmonds.

Residential Moving — Burnaby

Moving to or from a Burnaby house? We handle packing, loading, transport, and unpacking across South Burnaby, Burnaby Heights, and Capitol Hill.

Moving Cost in Burnaby

Vancouver

Movers in Vancouver

We serve all Vancouver neighbourhoods — Downtown, East Van, Kitsilano, Mount Pleasant, Kerrisdale, UBC, and the North Shore. Vancouver moves often require permit parking, narrow streets, and high-rise access — our team handles all of it.

Local Moving — Vancouver

Hourly local moves with a minimum two-person crew, covering all Vancouver neighbourhoods. No fuel surcharges within Metro Vancouver.

Apartment Moving — Vancouver

Packing Services — Vancouver

Full and partial packing across Vancouver. Our crew brings all materials — boxes, bubble wrap, wardrobe boxes, and mattress bags — and packs to professional standards.
