Moving Checklist

How to hire a moving company
in the Lower Mainland

Whether you're moving across Port Coquitlam or across BC, these are the steps every household should take before moving day.

1

Book an in-home estimate

Never accept a quote over the phone. A reputable moving company visits your home, walks every room, and gives you a written all-in price before any booking.

2

Verify licensing & insurance

In BC, movers must be licensed by the Passenger Transportation Board. Ask for proof of cargo and liability insurance. Check BBB accreditation — A+ is the highest rating available.

3

Confirm what's included

Ask specifically: Does the price include stairs, elevator time, and long carries? Are packing materials extra? Is fuel included? Hidden fees at delivery are the #1 complaint about movers in BC.

4

Book early — especially month-end

Month-end dates (25th–1st) and May–September are peak season in the Lower Mainland. The best moving companies fill up 3–4 weeks out. Request your estimate as soon as you have a date.

5

Prepare for moving day

Disassemble large furniture in advance, set aside items the movers won't handle (plants, valuables, documents), and reserve parking for the truck. A well-prepared home means a faster, cheaper move.

6

Consider storage if your dates don't align

Between closing dates? Containerized storage lets you load on your schedule — your belongings go into a secure, individual container at a monitored facility until your new home is ready.
