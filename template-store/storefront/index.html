FAQ

Questions, answered

How customizable are these templates, really?

Every color, font, radius and spacing value is a CSS variable defined once at the top of the stylesheet. Change a handful of values and the entire site restyles — no hunting through the file.

Do I need to know how to code?

Basic HTML/CSS familiarity helps for content edits, but the theme variables and included color presets mean most restyling needs zero coding.

Is this a subscription?

The template itself is a one-time purchase — you own the files outright. The hosted platform (dashboard, leads, customers, booking) is a separate, optional product with a 14-day free trial; nothing is charged automatically after the trial ends.

Can I use this for client projects?

The Business plan covers up to 5 domains. For unlimited client sites and white-label rights, choose the Agency Bundle.

What happens when my free trial ends?

Nothing gets deleted — you can still view everything you entered. You just can't add or edit until you upgrade, and there's no credit card on file to be charged automatically.
